01How it works

Three steps.
Zero awkward.

Setup takes under a minute. After that, every call is a new you.

01Upload

Drop in a character

A photo, a painting, an illustration. Anything with a face works. Build a library of up to 200 personas.

06Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Write to support@realfaceai.app.

Does my video leave my computer?

No. The swap runs on your own hardware and frames are not uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what account data we keep.

How are credits counted?

One credit is roughly one second of live streaming, and you’re only charged while a stream is live. Credits never expire.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Every plan works on multiple devices. Install the app on each Mac or Windows PC and sign in with the same account — they all share one credit balance.

Which apps does it work with?

Anything that can pick a camera: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Slack Huddles, OBS and browsers.

Whose face can I use?

Your own, a fictional character, or a real person who has given you explicit consent. Minors are never allowed. The Consent Policy has the full rules.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon or Intel, or 64-bit Windows 10/11 with a DirectX 12 GPU, plus 8 GB of RAM. Full details are on the download page.
