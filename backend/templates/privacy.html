Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data DeepFakes Softwares collects when you use RealFaceAI, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data. By using the platform, you consent to the practices described here.

02

Data We Collect

When you register and use RealFaceAI, we collect:

  • Email address and username (account creation)
  • Password (stored as a one-way hash — we never store your plain password)
  • IP address and device type (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Usage data (features used, credits consumed, session duration)
  • Payment records (transaction IDs and amounts — no card numbers are stored)
  • Images you upload for face swap features (processed in real-time and not stored permanently)

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

03

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve the RealFaceAI service
  • Process payments and manage your credit balance
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity
  • Send important service notifications (not marketing without consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations
04

Video and Image Processing

When you use real-time face swap features, your webcam feed is processed live. We do not permanently store your raw video stream. Session data is used only to deliver the AI output back to you in real-time and is discarded after the session ends. Uploaded reference images are held only for the duration of your session.

05

Third-Party Services

We use third-party infrastructure to deliver our service, including AI processing providers and cloud hosting. These providers process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements. We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

06

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain records (e.g. payment records for tax compliance, typically 7 years).

07

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@realfaceai.app.

08

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, and server-side API key management. No system is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but take every reasonable precaution to protect your data.

09

Cookies and Tracking

RealFaceAI is a desktop application. We do not use browser cookies. We may collect anonymised analytics (feature usage, error rates) to improve the product. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.

10

Children's Privacy

RealFaceAI is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us at support@realfaceai.app and we will delete the account.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests: support@realfaceai.app
